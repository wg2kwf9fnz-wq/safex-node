Solo Mining (Stratum)

Connecting to stratum server...
Connect a miner — everyone mines to their own address
stratum+tcp://—:17404

Your Safex address (only used to build the command below, it is not sent anywhere):

xmrig -a rx/sfx -o HOST:17404 -u YOUR_SAFEX_ADDRESS.rig1 -p x

The username is your Safex address, optionally followed by .workername and +difficulty (e.g. Safex5...abc.rig1+200000; default difficulty is 50,000). If one of your miners finds a block, the whole block reward goes to your address. This is solo mining: no pool, no fees, no payouts to manage. For miners outside your home network (e.g. MiningRigRentals) forward TCP port 17404 on your router to this Umbrel. Algorithm: RandomSFX (rx/sfx).
